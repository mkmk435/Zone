This manuscript is an extended and revised version of our SECRYPT 2026 paper, "On Automating the Obfuscation of Malware Payloads: Zone, a bin2bin Metamorphic Engine" (DOI: 10.5220/0015170600004103). The conference paper introduced the Zone architecture and Position-Independent Instruction design and evaluated native transformations on 102 Windows payloads. The present manuscript cites that work on its first page and in the introduction, identifies reused or adapted objects through their captions, and rewrites the title, abstract, conclusions, and explanatory prose. The historical study supplies the baseline; the detection plot is explicitly identified as a reanalysis of retained historical scan records with corrected configuration labels.

The extension adds code-data identification and shared data handling, distinct LLM classification and transformation paths, and integration and evaluation of assembly wrapping. Its new experiments include six annotated classification programs, 13 controlled functional programs, a comparison of data handling on 19 original payloads, wrapping and LLM campaigns covering all 102 original inputs, and a recovery comparison on 49 previously problematic inputs. It also provides an overall working-rate update, distinguishes build and functional outcomes, compares prompting conditions, and reports detection, diversity, and resource use. Sections 8-13 occupy approximately 13 of the manuscript's 30 pages (about 43% by page extent), providing substantial new methods, evaluation, and discussion beyond the requested 30% extension. This percentage is an approximate allocation of manuscript space, not a text-similarity measurement.
